# FinSight AI — System Limitations & Pilot Readiness

## 1. What FinSight AI Does NOT Do

To maintain scientific integrity and regulatory compliance, the following explicit boundaries are established:

1. **NO Live Money Movement:**
   FinSight AI has no connection to bank core payment rails, ACH, SWIFT, BEFTN, NPSB, bKash, Nagad, or card processing networks. It cannot debit, credit, transfer, or wire funds. All intervention execution operates strictly in `SANDBOX` or `SIMULATION` mode.
2. **NO Automated Underwriting or Credit Decisioning:**
   The model does not approve or deny credit, overdraft facilities, or loans. It does not calculate credit scores or make lending judgments under the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) or equivalent consumer credit regulations.
3. **NO Autonomous Cancellation of Obligations:**
   The agent is strictly prohibited from cancelling or pausing essential legal and financial obligations (rent, mortgage, utilities, taxes, insurance, debt service, healthcare, or employee payroll).
4. **NO Investment or Financial Advice:**
   The system provides operational cash-flow liquidity forecasting and voluntary budgeting suggestions. It is not an investment advisor, broker, or fiduciary.
5. **NO Unbounded LLM Decision Authority:**
   All financial probabilities, risk levels, cost matrices, and intervention selections are governed by deterministic Python logic. An LLM cannot override, hallucinate, or alter calculations.

---

## 2. Dataset Provenance & External Validation Status

- **Provenance:** The current demonstration environment is grounded entirely in **synthetic simulation benchmark data** generated by `scripts/generate_synthetic_data.py`.
- **External Customer Validation:**
  - **Zero real customer validation has occurred.**
  - The model and intervention policies have **NOT** been piloted on production bank accounts, live open banking APIs, or real consumers.
  - The reported performance metrics (PR-AUC 1.0, test cost 0.0) reflect perfect algorithmic convergence on clean synthetic Markovian generators. Real-world consumer ledger data contains significant unobserved heterogeneity, irregular cash deposits, shared household expenditures, and messy merchant tags that will degrade accuracy.

## 3. Technical & Modeling Limitations

1. **Unobserved Offline Cash Transactions:**
   In regions with high cash prevalence (such as Bangladesh), transactions conducted in physical cash or unlinked mobile wallets cannot be detected until an ATM withdrawal or explicit bank transfer occurs.
2. **Cold-Start Horizon Blind Spots:**
   Accounts with fewer than 14 days of history or fewer than 10 transactions lack sufficient statistical history to infer recurring bill cadences. The system flags these accounts with an `is_cold_start` indicator and suppresses high-confidence automated actions.
3. **Unscheduled Emergencies:**
   Sudden external financial shocks (medical emergencies, unexpected vehicular repairs, legal disputes) cannot be anticipated from historical transaction patterns alone.
4. **SHAP Observational Interpretation:**
   SHAP values quantify statistical feature attribution in model prediction space. They do **NOT** prove causal relationships in the real economy. For example, decreasing grocery spend by 2,000 BDT in reality might lead to higher dining spend elsewhere.

---

## 4. Requirements Before a Controlled Production Pilot

Before deploying FinSight AI to a controlled pilot with real human participants:

1. **Bank Partner Data Integration:**
   Secure OAuth2 / Open Banking API connectors with tokenized accounts and explicit consumer consent scopes.
2. **Regulatory & Compliance Audit:**
   Formal review by bank compliance and legal counsel regarding automated financial nudges, consumer protection statutes, and dark-pattern prevention.
3. **PII Hardware-Level Tokenization:**
   Replacement of local SQLite audit tables with an immutable enterprise ledger (e.g., PostgreSQL with AWS KMS / GCP Cloud KMS encryption).
4. **Consumer A/B Intervention Trial:**
   Randomized controlled trial (RCT) measuring whether FinSight AI advisories actually reduce real overdraft incidence and late-payment fees without inducing alert fatigue.
